Rules

New This Year
No gimmies on par or birdie putts. Anything for par or better has to be holed out. Bogey or worse, the group can give it.
New This Year
Improved lies. One club length, no closer to the hole, and you cannot move the ball from rough to fairway. Same cut of grass you found it in.
Round 5 Only
No concessions of any kind. Holes cannot be conceded and matches cannot be closed out early. Round 5 is also the final round of the individual, so everyone plays all 18 and holes out even if the match is already won. Your stableford points are live to the last putt.
  • 🚫 Max hole score is a gross triple bogey.
  • 📈 Handicaps are pulled from GHIN, 18Birdies, or assigned from previous trip rounds.
  • 📱 All scoring runs through Squabbit. Join the tournament before we tee off Thursday.
  • 👥 Pairings are set so everyone plays with everyone as much as the numbers allow. Some teammate overlap is unavoidable with 8 players.
